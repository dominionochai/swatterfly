## Dragonfly pursuit and interception

- Mischiati et al. (2015), *Nature* — [`nature.com/articles/nature14045`](https://nature.com/articles/nature14045); PubMed [`25487153`](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25487153). Evidence for a predictive internal model and body alignment to a future prey path.
- *Dragonflies use underdamped pursuit to chase* — [`journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/222/11/jeb190884`](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/222/11/jeb190884). Relevant to pursuit dynamics and damping comparisons.
- *Prey pursuit and interception in dragonflies* — [`researchgate.net/publication/12609048`](https://researchgate.net/publication/12609048). Relevant to interception behavior and biological comparison.

## Interpretation caveats

- L-type/K-type and `eta = psi_dot/(psi+c)` are **NOT verified canonical Gabbiani forms**. They are engineering notation or candidate abstractions and must not be presented as direct quotations of the canonical model.
- Dragonflies do not literally run missile PN. PN is a useful, interpretable engineering comparator for predictive interception; biological control should be described separately.
- DOI, PubMed, journal, repository, and preprint records should be rechecked before a formal paper or demo claim.
